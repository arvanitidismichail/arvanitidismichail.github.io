Dr Michail Arvanitidis

PT · PhD · MSc · MMACP

Postdoctoral Research Fellow, Centre of Precision Rehabilitation for Spinal Pain
School of Sport, Exercise and Rehabilitation Sciences, University of Birmingham

Birmingham, United Kingdom

I study how the nervous system controls muscle — and how that control breaks down in musculoskeletal pain and in extreme environments. My current work examines cervical spine and muscle adaptation in astronauts after spaceflight, alongside a broader programme using high-density surface EMG and motor unit analysis to understand, measure and retrain neuromuscular impairment in persistent pain.

Research Focus

Spaceflight & the cervical spine

Cervical spine and muscle adaptation after spaceflight and its relationship to disc herniation risk — the international Cervical in Space trial, funded by the UK Space Agency.

Neuromuscular control in pain

How persistent musculoskeletal pain alters force control, motor unit discharge and the spatial organisation of muscle activity — in the trunk, neck, knee and ankle.

HD-sEMG methods & biofeedback

Developing high-density EMG analysis pipelines, combined EMG–ultrasound recording, and real-time augmented-reality biofeedback to retrain muscle activation.
